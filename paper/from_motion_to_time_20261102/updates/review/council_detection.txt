WHAT IS WORTH INCLUDING
1. Evidence-based boundaries for the end-to-end claim. Detection is RGB MonoDGP-R50 plus a TRAIN-fitted shared logistic ranker, not an event detector or JEPA result. The later real eight-frame PHASE bridge yielded five signed TTCs in the last frame plus one track_not_confirmed. It proves functional causal integration only. Frozen TRAIN40 PHASE producers were exposed to these sequences; it does not demonstrate independent detector-to-TTC accuracy.
2. Fixed-pool ranking as an engineering control. On the same 23,958 development-exposed frames, eight classes, 190,469 raw GT and 1,197,900 proposals, baseline aligned mAP 0.05701725780005367 and ranked mAP 0.062042288605807405. Absolute gain 0.005025030805753735; no geometry/class/velocity change. Single detector seed, selection exposed. Bicycle and other_vehicle AP remain zero; motorcycle falls 0.04742940354287591 to 0.0457533024963028. No baseline submission exists, so cannot claim ranking improves official test.
3. Metric/population reconciliation is a useful methodological finding. Historical AP101 ranked 0.1850207831690803 was restricted-domain (107,308 eligible, 83,161 ignored), precision-envelope, different tie rules. On new raw matches, envelope formula gives 0.10937358 while aligned formula gives 0.06204229. Aligned AP uses ego-XY greedy class matching with strict <0.5/1/2/4 m, 101 recall interpolation without envelope, min_recall/min_precision=.1, macro over all eight classes. Private scorer's aggregate arithmetic was reconstructed, not private matching certified. Test AP 0.051295080474643054 on 35,940 frames is a separate official result; eAP composite .13980148 is not mAP. No new official submission in Nov1-2 diagnostic continuation.
4. Cuboid-edge inversion as a diagnostic, not priority novelty claim. For rotated corner offsets delta_i, center pixel u, and intrinsic K, a_i=K[0:2,:]delta_i-u*delta_iz. For positive edge distance t_e use signed numerators (-a_ix,+a_ix,-a_iy,+a_iy), z_e=max_i(signed_i,e/t_e-delta_iz). Median across non-clipped edges. This follows cuboid containment with the touching vertex; clipped borders are not equalities. With perfect target size/yaw/edges, the height prior H*fy/h has signed bias -4.844935754414118m, MAE 4.923050318241922m, vs inversion MAE 2.108504240207313e-6m on the same 25,424 targets from 4,096 TRAIN frames / 26,546 raw labels. This measures consistency with the pinhole target generator, not physical sensor calibration or learned accuracy.
5. Necessary counterexample: on a DIFFERENT 4,096-frame TRAIN query panel with 204,800 predicted proposals, baseline learned depth mAP .2106477859625314; height-only .08308034537667813; cuboid-edge-only .16478485385415936. Replacement keeps class/score/size/yaw/pool, removes learned depth residual, and falls back on invalid geometry (28 cuboid cases; 12 height cases). Cuboid inversion beats isolated height prior, but neither beats learned output. No model promotion, no heldout evaluation, no retraining compatible residual. Avoid implying near-zero ideal error translates to deployment.

LIMITATIONS AND EXCLUSIONS
- No SOTA, production/safety, calibrated uncertainty or end-to-end TTC generalization claim.
- Depth field historical name depth_log_variance actually parameterizes Laplace loss 1.4142*exp(-s)*abs(e)+s. Scale b=exp(s)/1.4142; do not use exp(s/2) Gaussian intervals.
- TRAIN census covers 70,279 frames in 24 sequences. Maximum optical depth 249.892971m; max radial ego XY 253.595616m. No TRAIN labels at 400m. 300m configuration does not imply capacity at 300/400m.
- Extra long-range targets overwhelmingly trucks (18,255 of 20,527 added beyond 150m); appearances are correlated and do not solve rare-class diversity.
- TRAIN/selection rare-class gaps do not isolate overfitting because identities and scene distribution differ. 24/8/8 split has no acquisition independence certification.
- Internal pinhole round-trip is not independent rectification audit. eAP remaps RGB to event plane; calibration approximation remains.
- MonoCoP recovery repeated 66 lost updates/132 exposures after host restart. Logical 4,096 exposures vs >=4,228 executed. Reported 3396.91s includes 3120s conservative reservation, not exact compute; losses not bitwise equal despite token replay.
- CI fixes, downloads, source-preserving formatter exceptions, cache throughput, technical smoke and restart plumbing belong in reproducibility record, not scientific novelty headline.

SUGGESTED APPENDIX PROSE
A separate RGB detection study exposes the limits of extending PHASE-TTC to predicted objects. On 23,958 public development frames, a TRAIN-fitted ranker increased aligned eight-class mAP from 0.0570 to 0.0620 while preserving proposal geometry. The submitted detector obtained official AP 0.0513 on 35,940 test frames; no paired baseline submission was made. The development set was reused for model selection, and rare-class performance remained weak. An ideal-target cuboid inversion removed the geometric bias of a height-only prior, but replacing learned depth with that inversion reduced TRAIN mAP from 0.2106 to 0.1648. These results support an auditable integration study, not a claim of improved end-to-end TTC accuracy.
