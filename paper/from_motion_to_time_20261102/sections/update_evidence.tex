\subsection{Review coverage and scientific selection}
The review fetched remote refs without modifying experiment branches and
inventoried 241 commits across 72 refs in the lookback beginning 28 October.
The 411 directly touched Markdown paths comprise 406 semantic or source-verified
documents and five generated historical inventories checked structurally.
The latter include older records outside the requested period. The manifest
distinguishes these categories and preserves divergent branch versions.

The council reviewed transfer and fusion, runtime, eAP detection, physical
measurement, and typography in separate lanes. A subsequent scientific review
checked the new manuscript's equations, metric units, evaluation populations,
causality, and unsupported-claim boundaries. These reviews guide inclusion;
they are not independent experimental replications. No new training or
official benchmark submission was performed to create this expanded article.
